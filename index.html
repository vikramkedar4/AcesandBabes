How to add a video
  1. Upload the video to YouTube (unlisted is fine) and copy its link.
  2. Open videos/videos.js in the GitHub repo and click the pencil to edit.
  3. Copy the example entry, remove the // at the start of each line, and fill in the title, link, date, and project.
  4. Commit. The site updates itself within a minute.
