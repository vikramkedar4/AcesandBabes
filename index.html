How it works

Three steps to glory

01

Form your couple

Each partner creates an account. One of you sends an invite by username, the other accepts. Your couple starts at 1200 ELO.

02

Log your matches

Play another couple at any game. Log the result. Nothing changes until the other couple confirms it, so there are no disputed wins.

03

Climb the ranks

Beat higher-rated couples to gain more ELO. Lose to lower-rated ones and pay for it. See where you stand on the global leaderboard.
